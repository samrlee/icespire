Rut introduced himself to Thom on the road to Phandalin as "Friend," and the
party has never learned anything better. His real name remains a mystery.

## Notable deeds

- Enjoyed mulled earth with Thom on the party's first night camping (Session 1).
- Got in Barnaby's face over breakfast for reasons known only to himself (Session 2).
- Stole a piece of bread from the Gnomengarde kitchen (Session 2).
- Freed the crazed gnome Facktoré mid-argument, letting her screech off into
  the wild — she is still at large (Session 2).
- Spent his last spell slot on Goodberry to patch the party up after the
  manticore fight, and ripped a tail spike from a manticore corpse. He kept
  it. He hasn't said why (Session 5).
- Absorbed a nasty acid hit with Absorb Elements, then Wild Shaped into a boar
  to tank the ochre jellies (Session 5).
- Fished a gold necklace out of a dead jelly's acidic remains with the Pole of
  Collapsing. Its inscription reads *"This is what happens to those who
  displease me."* He pocketed it. Nobody has flagged the risk (Session 5).
- Ran the rubble hallway as a rat, slipped past the last ochre jelly, and
  found the temple's third secret door — then failed, repeatedly and audibly,
  to open it (Session 6).
- Bit a dwarf prospector's ankle mid-argument while still a rat. Was not even
  noticed (Session 6).
- Dropped the rat shape at last and came back to himself still crouched on all
  fours and still squeaking, until the rest of the party stopped digging and
  stared at him (Session 6).
- Fought the orcs at the front line as a **wolf**, killed one outright with
  his teeth in its throat, and was knocked back out of the shape by an axe
  (Session 6).
- Spent everything he had left healing Barnaby off the floor — twice — and lay
  down beside him when it was over (Session 6).
- Sprang at the last orc's face and split its eye open with a dagger
  (Session 6).
