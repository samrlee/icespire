The party reached the mine about half a day after leaving Phandalin with
Don-Jon Raskin (Session 9). A tunnel lay among bushes at the foot of a
snow-capped mountain, with Mountain's Toe carved into a wooden board above it.

Don-Jon said he had paid for the place in Neverwinter but had never visited
and had no map. He demanded safety inside before providing Harbin's completion
note. Dax negotiated extra compensation: Don-Jon offered loose gold found
inside; Thom replied that they would take anything not bolted down. The wider
claim was not explicitly accepted.

Don-Jon led the party into the entrance as the session ended. No interior
features have been established, so the local map remains withheld. No escort
reward or completion note has been received.
