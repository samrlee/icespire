What is left of a stone windmill on a hill southeast of Phandalin — home and
workshop of the midwife-apothecary **Adabra Gwynn**, who brewed the town's
healing potions and refused for two sessions to leave it, dragon or no dragon.
The party fought off two manticores here in Session 4 and left with a free
healing potion and a promise to check in on the way back.

They kept the promise in Session 7, and the orcs finally persuaded her where
the dragon never had: she packed her brewing equipment and walked to Phandalin
with them. The **white dragon** destroyed Umbrage Hill that same evening.
Nothing is left of it. She is alive because they came back.
