A ruined settlement behind a broken black-stone gate, where dwarf prospectors
Norbus Ithermel and Dazlyn Grayshard were digging into a buried temple of
Abbathor — and where the god's transformed priests, now ochre jellies, blocked
the dig. Site of the 60/40 treasure split, the hidden gemstone coffer, and the
necklace that reads *"This is what happens to those who displease me."*

By the end of Session 6 all three jellies were dead, the prospectors had
walked out on the broken deal and stripped their camp, and **seven orcs** had
come up the canyon to claim the ruin as a fort — and died in the entrance
hall.

In Session 7 the party burrowed through the last rubble wall into a sealed
**round chamber**: two pedestals, two statues, one already in pieces, and a
booby-trapped emerald. It matches the ledger's sacrifice chamber, though
nothing in the room confirmed it. Working the gearing beneath
both pedestals opened a hidden alcove holding the temple's final offering
chest — 150 gold, 70 tarnished silver, and a written instruction to take it and
forget the god. The party left at dawn with all of it.

Two cave-ins in the eastern passage are still unexcavated, and the temple's
actual hoard has never been found.
