The party's home base — a frontier town of farmers and prospectors where
every job so far has started. Harbin Wester posts contracts from behind a
well-bolted door, the Miner's Exchange hides a Zhentarim guildmaster, and the
inn is where a strange figure first watched the party in Session 1.

As of Session 7 the town is fuller and edgier than the party has ever seen it:
the inn crowded with locals talking low about something nobody named, the
midwife evacuated in from Umbrage Hill, a dead dwarf carried through the door
to his brother, and — before the night is out — word that a dragon has levelled
the hill she came from. Harbin has "concerning reports" and "unfortunate work"
waiting for the party in the morning.
