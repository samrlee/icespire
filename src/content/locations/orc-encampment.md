Sage and Hamish scouted this large orc encampment a few miles down a split
trail in Session 5, after the party found day-old tracks of a sizable band.
The party bypassed it to reach the excavation — but orc activity is rising
across the region, and the camp is still there.

Seven orcs walked up the canyon to the temple the next day looking for "a good
new base." Whether they came from this camp, nobody asked and nobody will now
— all seven are dead. The camp itself remains unvisited and uncounted.
