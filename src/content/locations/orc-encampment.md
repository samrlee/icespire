Sage and Hamish scouted this large orc encampment a few miles down a split
trail in Session 5, after the party found day-old tracks of a sizable band.
The party bypassed it to reach the excavation — but orc activity was rising
across the region, and the camp sat there untouched for two sessions.

Seven orcs walked up the canyon to the temple the next day looking for "a good
new base." All seven are dead — and in Session 7 the tracks answered the
question of where they came from: no other orc sign crosses this ground, and
the seven came up from this camp.

From the rocky crag above the trail, Hamish and Sage watched the main body's
trail run flat and wide across the grassland and **away over the horizon to the
west**, well clear of the road north. Sage's panther, arriving from that
direction, confirmed it. The camp is empty, the band is somewhere west, and
nobody has ever counted them.
