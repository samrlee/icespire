A Colletite of Chauntea who lived and worked in the stone windmill at Umbrage
Hill — the one the party saved from two manticores. She is now in Phandalin,
and the windmill is gone.

- Refused Harbin's request to relocate: she insists she can't brew anywhere
  but her windmill, and her potion supply matters to the town (Session 5).
- Gave the party a free healing potion (Dax tossed it to a bleeding Thom) and
  a home-cooked meal (Session 5).
- Knows Harbin well; sent a note back with Sage confirming she's staying put
  (Session 5).
- Warned that orcs have grown more common in the area lately.
- The party agreed to check on her on the return trip — and did.

## The evacuation (Session 7)

The dragon had not moved her. The orcs did. Thom came back through her yard
with a dead dwarf over Barnaby's shoulder and told her about the encampment and
the seven that came up the canyon, and the color went out of her: she remembers
what orcs do here. A war band burned Phandalin to the ground about a **century
ago**, she said, and they have been picking at the region ever since. *Orcs are
quite devastating.*

She asked to come back with them, packed her brewing equipment, and walked to
Phandalin in the rain. She would not give up a bedsheet to wrap Dazlyn's body
in — she was not soiling her linens with his blood, dearie — but she found them
a burlap sack. She told Rut his orc eye was very good work and patted him on
the head.

That evening a runner brought word to the inn that **Umbrage Hill had been
destroyed by the dragon**. She is alive because the party came back for her.
Harbin Wester paid them 35 gold for it, in front of her.
