Guildmaster of Phandalin's Miner's Exchange — and, as her dragon-symbol pin
revealed, an agent of the **Zhentarim**. Dax's former contract employer.

- Received the King's sealed letter from Dax, closing out his original trade
  contract (Session 4).
- The letter's mention of the dragon prompted pointed questions; then came the
  pin, the crossbows, and a forced information-sharing agreement — the party
  keeps her informed on dragon intelligence, and she pays for updates
  (Session 4).
- Her guard outside is the same strange figure who watched the party at the
  inn back in Session 1.
- Let slip a lead: a ruined temple south along the **Trivial Trail**, where
  locals stash their gold (Session 4).
- Harbin keeps private notes on her — notes Bean now has.

## The night the news broke (Session 8)

The dragon's attack on Umbrage Hill reached her before the party did, and she
was delighted by it — *"our lovely visit from our reptile friend."* She would
not pay a premium for news the whole town already had, so Dax gave it to her
free and sold her orcs instead: where the band that attacked the excavation had
come from, that all seven were dead, and where the much larger force behind
them had gone. She had a map out on the desk before he finished and **paid 50
gold** for it.

Asked about Harbin, she said he had been afraid of this dragon a long while,
that the party had handed her *"a great opportunity"* — and then caught herself
and finished more carefully.

*Well, let's just say things may be looking up in Phandalin. And Harbin may no
longer be a part of it.*

On the way out she stopped being pleasant. If anyone is caught around the
Miner's Exchange at night again she knows where to find them, and she knows
where Harbin sends them, and she would **start with Thom**.

## What she said when she thought the room was empty

Thom and Bean were standing invisible in the middle of it.

> Well done. Well done on your scouting. Soon Harbin will be out and I will be
> town master. Those foolish adventurers have brought me everything I needed.

Then, to one of her guards: if any of the party are caught sniffing around
again, **they all die** — the entire troop set on them. Then she packed up, and
went down through a **trap door in the floor behind her desk**, and two guards
followed her, and the third locked up and went down after them. The door sealed
flush behind him. Nothing on the floor shows it was ever there.

She calls wherever it leads their **recon point**, and said they had a lot of
work to do that evening.

- **She is playing the party.** The information they have been selling her is
  what she is using to unseat Harbin; she said so in as many words, to people
  she trusted, in a room with two of them in it.
- Her ledgers record what she has paid out, **Dax's fee among them** — ordinary
  business, plainly kept, nothing illegal in any of it.
