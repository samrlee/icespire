One of two dwarf prospectors from Phandalin who hired the party to clear the
ochre jellies out of the ruined **Temple of Abbathor**. He is a miner, not a
fighter, and says so freely.

- Rattled by the party's dragon warning and the news of the nearby orc band —
  but refused to abandon the site (Session 5).
- Warmed to Dax after his formal introduction in Dwarvish (Session 5).
- Agreed to Thom's **60/40 treasure split** in the party's favor, plus food and
  lodging, in exchange for clearing the jellies (Session 5).
- Came for his share, was given dead bodies and rubble, took Bean's five gold
  pieces, and walked off the site with his brother (Session 6).
- Does **not** know about the coffer of gemstones, or the emerald.

## The road, and after (Session 7)

The orc band caught the brothers on the road north — the same seven, he
assumes, that went on to the temple. They killed **Dazlyn** before he could
react. Norbus ran, lived, and had been sitting in the Phandalin inn ever since.

The party reached him first through his brother's **sending stone**, and his
answer was five words longer than it needed to be: *Bring my brother back to
Phandalin. I will bury him. P.S. Pay me what you owe.* They carried the body
in and set it at his feet at the bar, and he broke down in front of the whole
room.

*We could have just settled this together. And then maybe my brother wouldn't
have died.*

He gave up the second sending stone — the pair had been a gift from his and
Dazlyn's parents for the brothers' new mining venture, and he had no use for
half of it now. The party paid him **25 gp and 70 tarnished silver** against
the broken split; the gold came out of Thom's and Dax's own purses, and the bag
sat up fat enough in his hand that he thought it was more than it was. He
called it decent, thanked them, and asked to be left alone. They had more
dwarves to swindle, he supposed.

He was not repulsed by Rut's orc eye. He said it was good work, and hugged him.

## Dax's arrangement

After the others had drifted toward the door, **Dax** went back alone and put
down a second bag — **15 gp** on top of what the party had paid — and told him
that if he ever came across another job that needed a sword, to come find him.

*What was your name again, sir?*

Norbus has the name. The rest of the party has no idea any of this happened.
