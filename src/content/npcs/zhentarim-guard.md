The strange person the party noticed watching them at the Phandalin inn on
their very first night in town (Session 1). Nothing came of it at the time.

Three sessions later, the same figure was standing guard outside the Miner's
Exchange while Guildmaster Thornton leveled crossbows at the party — one of
her Zhentarim people, and proof the Black Network had eyes on the party from
the start (Session 4).

## There are more of them (Session 8)

On the night the dragon news broke, three of the party followed **a** dark-clad
man through the alleys to the back of the Miner's Exchange. Whether he was this
one, nobody can say — they dress alike and keep to the shadows, and the party
has never seen a face.

What Session 8 established is how many there are. **A second** came around the
building, heard Dax's chain mail, caught him, recognised him as *"that
sellsword"*, and walked him in to the guildmaster rather than running him off.
**Three** were in the room with her. When Sage was found in the lane with a
weasel at her feet, she was believed and sent on her way — and then the guard
looked down, said what people say about vermin, and threw a knife at the
weasel. He missed.

And Thornton, giving orders she thought no one else could hear, referred to
**"our entire troop"** — which she is willing to set on the party if any of
them are caught around her building at night again.
