Two dwarf prospectors from Phandalin excavating the ruined **Temple of
Abbathor** behind a broken black-stone gate in a canyon southwest of town.

- Rattled by the party's dragon warning and the news of the nearby orc band —
  but refused to abandon their site (Session 5).
- Warmed to Dax after his formal introduction in Dwarvish (Session 5).
- Agreed to Thom's **60/40 treasure split** in the party's favor, plus food
  and lodging, in exchange for clearing the ochre jellies blocking their dig
  (Session 5).
- Shared the temple's history and warned of secret doors.
- Do **not** know about the coffer of gemstones Dax found in the pillar base.

## The deal breaks (Session 6)

With the jellies cleared, they came for their share and were given dead
bodies, rubble, and no gold at all. They heard the shape of Dax's lie before
he finished it, and Barnaby's attempt at intimidation went nowhere. Only Thom
— loud, fast, and generous with detail — was believed.

They put their hands out anyway: *we had an agreement, sixty-forty, we'll let
it slide — split it now.* Bean paid them **five gold pieces** out of her own
purse. They looked at the coins, at the party, and at a temple full of doors
these adventurers had spent all day opening. Then they stamped out through the
entrance hall and were gone.

By the time Sage went outside to try to talk them around, the camp was
stripped bare — bedrolls, tools, the fire kicked apart and cold. They left
without a word, and the party has no idea where they went. Two dwarves from
Phandalin now know exactly what this party is worth as a partner, and the
gemstone coffer is still undeclared.
