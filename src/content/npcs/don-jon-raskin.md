A human in a leather jacket and an upturned-brim hat, waiting at the Phandalin
inn for Harbin's adventurers. The party escorted him to Mountain's Toe Gold
Mine in Session 9 for a promised **150 gp**, not yet paid.

On the road he told stories about Neverwinter, Neverwinter Wood, Baldur's Gate
and a horde of mimics. These are his boasts, not feats the party witnessed.
The six cold-damaged orc bodies on the approach unsettled him.

At the entrance he said he had paid for the mine in Neverwinter but had never
been inside and had no map. He wanted the party to make it safe before he
provided a completion note for Harbin. When they objected that this exceeded
the escort, he threatened consequences for Harbin through his employers.
Thom accused him of being Zhentarim; he did not confirm it.

Dax negotiated a separate arrangement. Don-Jon offered **whatever loose gold
they found inside**; Thom answered that they would take anything not bolted
down. Acceptance of that broader claim is not established. No gold has been
recovered and no completion note given.

Bean called him stupid in Halfling. He appeared not to understand. Dax asked
whether he could fend for himself in a fight, and Don-Jon led the way into the
mine with the party following. That is where the session ended.
