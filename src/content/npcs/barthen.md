The proprietor of Barthen's Provisions. Thom bought scroll-making supplies and
a component pouch from him for **35 gp** instead of fifty, on condition that
he tell his friends where to shop (Session 9).

When the party wanted to avoid trading with Holia Thornton, Harbin suggested
Barthen as a buyer with good standing in town. He warned that Barthen might
sell the gems on to her; no onward sale has been recorded.

Barthen paid **150 gp** for the small gems and **350 gp** for the great emerald.
The sale did not identify the emerald's properties.
