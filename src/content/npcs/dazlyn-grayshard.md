The other half of the dwarf prospecting pair at the **Temple of Abbathor** —
Norbus Ithermel's brother, and a partner in the mining venture their parents
had outfitted them for.

- Negotiated down to a **60/40 split** in the party's favor and put the pair's
  camp and provisions behind it (Session 5).
- Was on the receiving end of the party's lie about the haul, asked for the
  split anyway, took Bean's five gold, and left the dig with his brother
  (Session 6).

## Found on the trail (Session 7)

The party came on him face down on the road north, an orc javelin standing out
of his back, killed before he could react by the same band that came up the
canyon after them. One hand was closed hard around a **sending stone** — its
pair was in Norbus's pocket, and he had been trying to use it when he died.

Hamish pried his fingers open. Barnaby carried him. Adabra Gwynn gave up a
burlap sack for hauling herbs, and his boots stuck out of the end of it; Sage
gathered flowers from the field and Rut opened the buds, and they laid them on
top.

They left him in a bush outside the Quest Master's house, in the rain, while
they collected their pay — then carried him into the inn and set him down at
his brother's feet.
