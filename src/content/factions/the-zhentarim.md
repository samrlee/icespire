An unscrupulous shadow network expanding its influence across Faerûn —
ambitious, opportunistic, and meritocratic. Publicly a mercenary and
protection outfit; secretly running smuggling, extortion, and worse.

## The party's entanglement

- **Guildmaster Holia Thornton** of Phandalin's Miner's Exchange wears the
  Zhentarim dragon pin. Hamish recognized the mark (Session 4).
- Her guard — the strange figure who watched the party at the inn back in
  Session 1 — is one of her people. The Network had eyes on the party from
  their first night in town.
- At crossbow-point, Thornton pressed the party into an **information-sharing
  pact**: all dragon intelligence flows to her, and she pays for updates
  (Session 4).
- Dax, whose original contract was with Thornton, remains pragmatically
  willing to keep working with her so long as coin is on the table.
- Bean holds Harbin's stolen private notes on Thornton — contents so far
  unexamined at the table.

## What Session 8 exposed

The pact was never a trade. **The dragon intelligence the party has been selling
Thornton is what she is using to take Phandalin**, and she said so to her own
people in a room she believed was empty: soon Harbin will be out, she will be
town master, and the foolish adventurers have brought her everything she needed.

The Network's footprint in town is bigger than one guildmaster and one watcher
at the inn:

- **Four of them** in the Exchange that night, plus the man the party tailed
  there. Thornton speaks of setting **"our entire troop"** on people.
- A **trap door in the floor behind her desk**, which she and three guards went
  down and which then sealed flush — no seam, no handle, nothing to say it had
  ever been there. She calls what is down there their **recon point**, and said
  they had a lot of work to do that evening.
- **Standing orders on the party**: caught around the building at night again
  and they all die, starting with Thom.

Dax sold her the orc band's position and heading for 50 gold the same night.
What she wants with a large orc force moving west is not clear.
