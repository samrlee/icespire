The frontier town at the center of the campaign, living in the shadow of the
white dragon rumors.

**Known locations:**

- **The quest board & Quest Master** — where the work comes from.
- **Harbin Wester's house** — the townmaster pays out here, behind many locks.
- **The inn** — limited gnome intel, and at least one Zhentarim watcher among
  the patrons.
- **The Miner's Exchange** — run by Guildmaster Holia Thornton, who is more
  than she appears. There is a **trap door in the floor behind her desk** that
  closes flush into the boards and cannot be found from above; she and her
  guards go down it at night, to what she calls their **recon point**.
- **The Lionshield Coster** — arms and armor. Locked at night, open the next
  morning for Barnaby's bow, Dax's shield and Hamish's whip. Shield decoration
  was offered but not commissioned (Session 9).
- **Barthen's Provisions** — general supplies. Barthen discounted Thom's scroll
  supplies and component pouch in exchange for recommendations, then bought
  the small gems and great emerald for **500 gp** altogether (Session 9).
- **The burned houses** — several buildings still standing open to the weather
  from a raid the town has not repaired. One of them makes a serviceable
  hiding place.

**Regional concerns:** rising orc activity (Adabra's warning, the tracks and
encampment near the excavation), and the white dragon, which stopped being a
rumor when it destroyed Umbrage Hill — taking with it the windmill Phandalin's
entire supply of healing potions depended on.

**Who runs the town is now in question.** Harbin Wester is the townmaster and
the banker both. Guildmaster Thornton intends to have the office, has told her
own people so, and is using the party's dragon intelligence to get it.

In Session 9 Thom and Bean returned from below the Exchange and told the
whole party about the traps, occupied hall, Thornton's office and second
escape hatch. Their readings of her wider plans remained interpretations.
Three hooded figures searched the inn; the innkeeper kept the alibi Thom had
paid **3 gp** for. The party slept undisturbed.

Harbin learned the tunnels were still used and paid **300 gp** for the
intelligence and entrance information. After shopping and selling the gems,
the party left with Don-Jon Raskin for Mountain's Toe Gold Mine. The logging
delivery and reported attack at Butterskull Ranch remain future work.
