A stone windmill outside Phandalin, home and workshop of midwife-apothecary
**Adabra Gwynn**, who brews the healing potions supplied to the town.

The party arrived on Harbin's contract to bring Adabra to safety and instead
found **two manticores** tearing the place apart (Session 4). After a long,
scrappy fight — fog cloud, magic missiles, handaxes, sneak attacks, an Action
Surge — Bean and Sage put the manticores down.

The windmill survived that. Mostly. Adabra stayed: she couldn't brew anywhere
else, and the town needed the potions. The party promised to check on her on
the return trip.

## Destroyed (Session 7)

They kept the promise. Coming back from the temple with a dead dwarf, they told
her about the orc encampment and the seven raiders — the threat the dragon had
never been to her — and she packed her brewing equipment and walked to
Phandalin with them in the rain.

That same evening a man ran into the inn with a report he had just been handed:
**Umbrage Hill had been attacked. It was a dragon. There was nothing left of
it.**

The party did not witness the attack or inspect the site afterward. Adabra is
in Phandalin with her brewing equipment; no replacement workshop has been
established.
