---
title: 'The Final Offering Note'
category: 'history'
summary: 'A note from the Temple of Abbathor: an instruction to stop paying the god, and permission for whoever survived the room to take what was left. Its author is unknown.'
---

Found in Session 7, folded in with the coin in the locked chest in the hidden
chamber's alcove. Hamish opened the wall by working the gearing sunk beneath
both pedestals. Thom had already lifted the emerald with Mage Hand and
detonated the standing statue.

> May all offerings to Abbathor forever cease. If you have found this room and
> you have survived, take what remains. Let the memory of Abbathor be
> forgotten.

Unlike the **ledger**, it gives no account of offerings. It calls for them to
stop, tells whoever survived the room to take what remains, and asks that
Abbathor be forgotten. The party does not know who wrote it or when.

## The open question

**It is written in Common.** Not Dwarvish — not the older dialect the ledger
uses, not the runes cut into the holy symbol or acid-etched over the necklace.
The party has not learned why its author chose that language.

Nobody in the party has asked why. Thom showed the note around chiefly to
establish that they had been invited to rob the place.
