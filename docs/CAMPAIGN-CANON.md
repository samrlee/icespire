# Campaign canon

A concise reference for humans and AI tools: durable in-world facts established
through play, checked against Sessions 0–9. This is a reference to the repository,
not an independent source that overrides it.

## Using and maintaining this file

- Update after a session when new durable facts are established. Prefer concise
  factual statements, with the establishing session cited when useful; update
  changed ownership or knowledge rather than leaving contradictory snapshots.
- Do not add speculation, jokes as literal world facts, inferred motives,
  narrative embellishment, or undiscovered module material. A hidden location
  file, map, creature illustration, or image filename does not establish that
  the party encountered or learned about its subject.
- Preserve who witnessed, heard, believed, or was told something. A fact available
  to readers or players is not automatically known by their characters. Record
  an NPC's report as a report and a character's belief as a belief.
- Record completed actions separately from promises: agreed is not paid,
  offered is not accepted, and a body returned for burial is not a witnessed burial.
- Keep material source disagreements under **Canon questions / conflicts** for
  Samuel; do not silently choose a version or change existing content to fit.
  When he rules on one, correct the sources it names and record the ruling there
  so the question is not reopened.
- [TABLE-FACTS.md](TABLE-FACTS.md) remains authoritative for players, character
  identities, the DM, house rules, names, and transcript interpretation.
  [The campaign summary](../src/pages/campaign.md) owns the living current state
  and open threads. This file retains accumulated facts and consequential
  knowledge boundaries, rather than duplicating that session-by-session summary.
- Apply [WRITING-STYLE.md](WRITING-STYLE.md)'s distinction between events and
  interpretation, and [SESSION-WORKFLOW.md](SESSION-WORKFLOW.md)'s knowledge and
  payment checks. Recheck the recaps and affected entity files after an update.

Session references below mean the corresponding files in
[the session collection](../src/content/sessions/). Supplemental facts that are
more specific than the recaps cite their entity source explicitly. Knowledge
and possession statements are last verified through Session 9, not guarantees
about subsequent play.

## NPCs and companions

- **Harbin Wester:** Phandalin's townmaster, banker, and quest-giver. Adabra
  knows him well. He admitted withholding the dragon news to avoid panic and
  people leaving town. He said Phandalin cannot defend itself and that all his
  jobs for the party were searches for a weapon to kill the dragon, without
  success. These are his stated reasons and assessment. (Sessions 4–5, 8)
- **Holia Thornton:** guildmaster of the Miner's Exchange and a Zhentarim agent.
  Received King Korboz's sealed reply from Dax. She has explicitly declared her
  intention to replace Harbin as townmaster; this is a plan, not an accomplished
  change of government. She threatened the party and gave her guards conditional
  orders to kill them. The witnesses differ; see the knowledge section.
  (Sessions 4, 8)
- **Adabra Gwynn:** midwife, apothecary, and Colletite of Chauntea. Brewed
  Phandalin's healing potions at Umbrage Hill. Said she could not brew elsewhere,
  initially refused evacuation, gave the party a healing potion, and sent Sage
  with a note confirming she would stay. The orc threat later persuaded her to
  pack her brewing equipment and accompany the party to Phandalin, before the
  reported destruction of her windmill. (Sessions 4–5, 7)
- **Norbus Ithermel and Dazlyn Grayshard:** brothers, dwarf prospectors from
  Phandalin, and partners at the excavation. Their parents gave them a pair of
  sending stones for the venture. They left after the treasure dispute. Dazlyn
  was found dead with an orc javelin in his back, reaching for his sending stone, killed by the same seven that later attacked the temple. Norbus said
  orcs killed his brother and he escaped. The body was left in a bush outside
  Harbin's house while the party collected their pay, then Barnaby carried it
  into Phandalin's inn and set it at Norbus's feet. Norbus said he would bury
  Dazlyn; the burial itself is not recorded. (Sessions 5–7)
- **King Korboz and Queen Gnerkli:** co-rulers of Gnomengarde. The party saw the
  Queen bound while the King checked objects for mimics; two mimics were then
  defeated. Korboz received the trade contract and gave Dax a sealed reply.
  [Gnerkli's entry](../src/content/npcs/queen-gnerkli.md) additionally records
  Korboz freeing her and their resumption of joint rule. (Session 3)
- **Facktoré:** Gnomengarde tinkerer who shot Thom with a crossbow ballista.
  Sage and Hamish partly disabled it; Thom put her to sleep, Dax bound her,
  and Bean took her ten gold. Rut released her and she fled. No subsequent
  encounter is recorded. (Session 2)
- **Phantom:** Sage's black panther companion, met on the road in Session 7 and
  first named aloud in Session 8. Told Sage he had been asking local animals
  where to find her. Follows her commands and scouts for her. Understands her
  without a spell; she needs Speak with Animals to understand his answers.
  See [Phantom](../src/content/npcs/phantom.md) for the recorded companion rules.
- **Thornton's guards:** the watcher at the inn in Session 1 was identified as
  her guard in Session 4. Three guards were observed in her office in Session
  8. The party cannot reliably distinguish the similarly dressed men; do not
  identify the man followed that night as the original watcher.

## Locations, factions, and important enemies

- **Phandalin:** the party's base, with a quest board, inn, Harbin's house,
  Miner's Exchange, Lionshield Coster, town-square shrine, and unrepaired burned
  houses. The Coster sells arms and armor; Barnaby found it locked at night, and the
  party shopped there the next morning. Barthen's Provisions supplied Thom
  and bought the party's gems. (Session 9)
  Do not equate the town-square shrine with the undiscovered Shrine of Savras.
  (Sessions 1, 4, 7–8; [Phandalin](../src/content/lore/phandalin.md))
- **Wayside camp:** the overnight stop on the Gnomengarde road where the party
  killed a wild boar and preserved it for food. Dax was met nearby the next
  morning. (Sessions 1–2; [camp](../src/content/locations/wayside-camp.md))
- **Gnomengarde:** reclusive gnome tinkerers and mushroom farmers ruled by Korboz
  and Gnerkli. The party saw mushroom oil, bread and wine, crab-like machines,
  hidden passages, a rope bridge, and a blade trap they disabled. Defeated **two
  mimics** in the storage room and completed the trade delivery. Thom told
  guards mimics could not become people; that statement is not a general
  bestiary ruling. (Sessions 2–3)
- **Umbrage Hill:** Adabra's stone windmill and potion workshop. The party killed
  **two manticores** attacking it; Bean and Sage landed the final blows. The
  windmill survived that fight. A messenger later reported it completely
  destroyed by the dragon, the same day Adabra was evacuated. The party did not
  witness the attack or inspect its aftermath. No replacement potion workshop
  is established. (Sessions 4–5, 7)
- **Dwarven Excavation / Temple of Abbathor:** the same site, a ruined dwarven
  settlement behind a broken black-stone gate in a canyon southwest of
  Phandalin. Its three original ochre jellies and all seven attacking orcs were
  killed; the prospectors abandoned the dig. (Sessions 5–7)
- **Orc encampment:** Sage and Hamish scouted a large camp down a split trail;
  the party bypassed it. On the return journey its main body had moved west,
  clear of the road north, corroborated by the panther's report. No exact force
  size is established. (Sessions 5, 7)
- **Orc bands:** Dax brought orc rumors of a dragon. The seven who attacked the
  temple said they wanted it as a fort/new base. Their leader recognized Dax
  as responsible for orc deaths near Gnomengarde; all seven died. Neither a
  complete command structure nor a common motive for every band is established.
  (Sessions 2, 6)
- **The white dragon:** the subject of Harbin's warnings and the reported attack
  on Umbrage Hill. The party has not seen it in person. Dax and Thornton reasoned
  from the attack's timing that it was a short flight from Phandalin; that does
  not locate its lair. No personal name or lair discovered in play is recorded.
  (Sessions 1–2, 4, 7–8; [dragon entry](../src/content/npcs/the-white-dragon.md))
- **The Zhentarim:** Thornton and her guards belong to the network whose dragon
  pin Hamish recognized. Thornton coerced the intelligence pact with crossbows
  trained on the party. Her reference to an “entire troop” was followed by Thom and Bean
  reporting roughly twenty to thirty people in the underground hall. This is
  their estimate, not a roster or a confirmed alliance with the orcs.
  (Sessions 4, 8–9)
- **The Miner's Exchange:** has a hidden hatch behind Thornton's desk that seals
  flush. Thom and Bean watched Thornton and three guards descend, then Bean
  opened its catch with thieves' tools. They reached stone below by ladder.
  Thornton called their destination a **recon point**. In Session 9 they told
  the party of a long mining passage, bear trap, arrow pressure plate that hit
  both of them, tripwire Bean kept Thom from crossing, occupied hall, maps,
  untouched gold and chests, and Thornton's private office. They escaped via
  another ladder before invisibility ended; the hatch was heard closing.
  Its surface position and the full tunnel layout are unrecorded. Smuggling,
  a foothold along the Sword Coast and Phandalin as a base remain Thom's
  interpretations of overheard fragments and document headings. (Sessions 8–9)
- **Trivial Trail temple:** Thornton reported a ruined temple south along that
  trail where locals stash gold. This remains an unvisited lead, not verified
  treasure or another name for the Temple of Abbathor. **Leilon**, the **Triboar
  Trail**, and the **High Road** also appeared on the orc map; a map reference
  is not a visit. (Sessions 4, 7)

## Session 9 arrivals and discoveries

- **Barthen:** proprietor of Barthen's Provisions, a neutral merchant. Discounted
  Thom's supplies and component pouch to **35 gp** for recommendations, then
  bought both lots of gems. Harbin suggested he might resell them to Thornton;
  no such resale was observed.
- **Don-Jon Raskin:** human escort client in a leather jacket and upturned-brim
  hat. His travel and mimic-fighting stories are boasts. He said he paid for
  the mine and answers to employers; they remain unnamed. Thom's Zhentarim
  accusation was not confirmed. No faction is assigned.
- **Mountain's Toe Gold Mine:** reached after about half a day's travel from
  Phandalin. A signed tunnel entrance among bushes at a snow-capped mountain's
  foot. Don-Jon led the party in at the close; no interior features or occupants
  were established. Keep the local interior map withheld.
- **Six dead orcs:** on the approach, not assigned an exact mapped position.
  Bean, Hamish and Rut examined them after Thom's inconclusive attempt. Cold
  injuries looked like white-dragon work. No attack, dragon or direction of
  travel was witnessed.
- **Loggers' Camp and Butterskull Ranch:** heard of in Harbin's job notices,
  neither visited. Harbin's unnamed half-brother needs provisions at the camp;
  the ranch was reportedly attacked by orcs. The withdrawn Woodland Manse and
  Falcon briefing establishes no discoveries.

## Established lore and discoveries

- **Abbathor:** the dwarves explained that the god of greed, displeased with his
  priests' sacrifices, buried the settlement in an avalanche and transformed
  surviving priests into ochre jellies. They knew no way to restore them. This
  is the history given to the party, not an event they witnessed. (Session 5)
- **Jellies:** the party observed slashing and lightning splitting them. Dax's
  thrown handaxe split one; Barnaby's lightning breath split another. All three
  original jellies, including their split portions, were killed. (Sessions 5–6)
- **Ledger:** records generations of gold, gems and offerings, diminishing over
  time, with defensive references to investments. Describes a dark sacrifice
  chamber flanked by two statues. Does not account for where the wealth went.
  Sage read it privately; no complete party briefing is recorded. (Session 6)
- **Temple discoveries:** hidden passages include a connection back to the main
  chamber. Explored rooms contained a crypt skeleton, two dead dwarves in red
  vestments, a dry font, and a sleeping chamber behind it. Some cave-ins remain
  unexcavated. Do not turn the prose's “every hidden passage” into knowledge of
  unexplored geometry. (Session 6; [temple](../src/content/lore/temple-of-abbathor.md))
- **Round chamber:** Rut opened access through rubble. One pedestal held a
  horned-dwarf statue and emerald; the other statue was already fragmented.
  Removing the emerald detonated the standing statue and injured Rut and
  Barnaby. Hamish operated gearing beneath both pedestals to open the alcove;
  Bean unlocked its chest. It held **150 gp and 70 silver**, plus the offering
  note. (Session 7)
- **Limits of that discovery:** the chamber's identity as the ledger's sacrifice
  chamber was never confirmed. The matching rubble patterns do not establish
  who broke the earlier statue, when, or precisely how. No second gem was
  recovered or witnessed in place. The note does not establish its author's
  identity, a divine blessing, or freedom from consequences.
- **Phandalin's destruction:** Adabra said an orc war band burned the town about
  a century ago and orcs continued raiding the region. Attribute this history
  to her; it does not establish her age or that she personally witnessed it.
  Burned houses are still present. (Sessions 7–8)

## Relationships, agreements, and completed payments

- **Gnomengarde commissions completed:** the party delivered the warning and
  trade contract; Dax returned Korboz's sealed reply to Thornton, closing his
  original commission. Harbin paid **135 gp** and returned the wand.
  (Sessions 1–4)
- **Dwarf agreement:** Thom negotiated **60% to the party, 40% to the brothers**,
  plus food and lodging, for clearing the jellies. The party concealed treasure;
  Bean gave them **5 gp** and they left. Returning Dazlyn, Thom contributed
  **10 gp** and Dax **15 gp**, plus the chest's **70 silver**, to Norbus. He
  accepted and thanked them without knowing about the gems or emerald. Do not
  treat that acceptance as an informed accounting of the agreed share.
  (Sessions 5–7; character and Norbus entries)
- **Dax and Norbus, private:** afterward Dax gave **another 15 gp** from his own
  purse and offered his sword if Norbus found work requiring it. Norbus took
  his name. No later job is established. (Session 7)
- **Adabra and excavation jobs completed:** advertised at **25 gp** and **50 gp**;
  Harbin actually paid **35 gp** and **70 gp**. He was told the dwarves “left,”
  not that Dazlyn died. (Sessions 4, 7)
- **Thornton's intelligence pact:** coerced agreement to provide dragon news in
  exchange for payment. In Session 8 Dax negotiated an information trade before
  sharing the orc intelligence; she paid him **50 gp** for positions/movements
  and answered questions about Harbin. This payment is separate from the
  original trade commission. Dax disclosed the negotiation, specific threat against Thom and payment in
  Session 9; the fifty gold was shared at **7 gp each**.
- **Split remainders are not tracked.** 19 gp each of 135 leaves 2 gp; 36 gp
  each of 255 leaves 3 gp. Samuel confirms these are rounding at the table and
  go nowhere. Do not assign them a holder.
- **Harbin's investigation fee:** raised from **90 gp to 100 gp** for information
  about Thornton's plans in Session 8. In Session 9 he paid **200 gp** for the
  intelligence and **100 gp** for entrance information, **300 gp total**,
  settling this job. No separate additional hundred remains due. His request for overnight
  guards was declined by Bean; no guarding agreement was concluded. (Sessions 8–9)

- **Session 9 sales and division:** Barthen's **150 gp** for small gems and
  **350 gp** for the emerald joined Harbin's **300 gp**, making **800 gp** shared
  at **114 gp each**. No new payment to Norbus; the sale did not settle his
  uninformed share. Remainders go nowhere, as before.
- **Three new jobs:** **150 gp each** for the mine escort, logging provisions
  and ranch investigation. Mine first, then a planned return for the cart;
  the other two journeys and collection of supplies have not happened. Harbin
  called the mine work part of his Neverwinter arrangement. No reward paid yet.
- **Mine-entry dispute:** Don-Jon demanded safety inside before a completion
  note, threatening consequences for Harbin through his employers. Dax secured
  an offer of loose gold found inside; Thom's broader claim to anything not
  bolted down was not explicitly accepted. No treasure or note received.
- **Alibi and rest:** Thom paid the innkeeper **3 gp**. Three hooded figures
  searched the inn; the innkeeper supported the alibi. Phantom reported the
  search to Sage without knowing its target. The whole party slept undisturbed
  and received a long rest. Dax exercised in his room, not outside; Samuel
  confirms a temporary **+1 to Dexterity checks the next day**. No level-up.

## Canon questions / conflicts

No blocking contradictions remain after Samuel's September 19 clarifications.
The escape hatch's surface position, exact entrance information sold to Harbin,
and the identity/holder of the transcript's “priest's medallion” remain unknown.
The medallion reference does not establish a transfer or sale of either known

### Rulings on record

Samuel ruled on these on 2026-09-17. The sources named have been corrected to
match; the rulings are kept so the same questions are not reopened.

- **Orc approach: west.** The seven came up from the encampment west of the
  ruin, as Session 7's tracks showed. Dax's "from the east" in Session 8 was
  Samuel misremembering out of role, not an in-character error — Dax would have
  known the ground — so Session 8 and the campaign summary now read west.
- **Knife thrown at Rut: the guard.** The campaign summary's sentence made Sage
  the thrower. Corrected there; Session 8 and the character entries were right.
- **Dazlyn's death: confirmed, not assumed.** Owen confirmed both that the
  temple's seven killed him and that he was reaching for the sending stone.
  Session 7 and Norbus's entry no longer hedge it as Norbus's assumption.
- **Exchange headcount: unresolved in world.** Thornton and three guards were in
  the room. Whether the man the party tailed was one of the three or a fourth is
  not knowable to the party, and the Zhentarim entry now says so instead of
  counting him twice.
- **Temple longsword: September 17 ruling superseded.** The earlier ruling
  called it mundane. On **September 19**, reviewing Session 9, Samuel explicitly
  corrected it to a **+1 longsword with no additional special properties**.
  Barnaby sold his ordinary sword and retained the temple blade. Session 6's
  loot callout, the haul and his character entry now reflect the correction;
  it was not a newly performed identification in Session 9. The holy symbol
  remains unidentified.
- **Ochre jellies: slashing and lightning only.** Bludgeoning was never tested
  in play, and Dax's *thrown* handaxe split one — so there is no thrown-weapon
  exception. Both tactical notes now record only what the party saw.
- **Coin remainders: nowhere.** Rounding at the table; the 2 gp and 3 gp are not
